- Attacked by vines in a pyramid shaped room

- The party collected 6 ***dagger shaped thorns***
- Travelled to a room with a "fountain"

    - The two streams flanked a carved statue with its mouth open
    - The part was followed into the room by spider moths
    - Honey-Mae and Banjo were put to sleep by little eyeball fish swimming in the pool (they remained asleep for at least 30 minutes, until someone decided to try and wake them up)
    - From beyond the room, the voice of Nisaba spoke
    - She demanded offerings of sentimental value in exchange for answers to questions
    - After exhausting their items, the party moved to a new room with two large anthropomorphic statues (A goat and a deer)
    - At the base of one statue a hidden door was found
    - It led to a room with a trapped treasure chest, triggering it caused arrows to shoot out of little holes in the wall
    - Everyone laid flat on their stomachs to avoid the arrows while Calypso opened the chest
    - Due to their ingenious plan, everyone avoided taking a hit from the arrows save for Calypso 
    - The treasure chest contained a magic cloak that causes the wearer to "dissapear, leaving a pile of rocks in their place"
    - The treasure chest also revealed a hidden passageway to a library(?)
